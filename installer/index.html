Retro for your Solana badge

Play Game Boy, Game Boy Color and NES games on your Breakpoint 2026 badge. Two clicks, nothing to install on your computer.

This browser can't talk to the badge. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer (Windows, Mac or Linux).

Before you start

  • A computer with Chrome or Edge.
  • Your badge with its microSD card inside, and a USB cable that carries data (some only charge).
  • Your games. None are included: use games you copied from your own cartridges, or free homebrew games.
Step 1

Install Retro

Plug in the badge, click the button and pick USB-SERIAL CH340. Your badge keeps its Wi-Fi, badge ID and store apps. Only the operating system is updated.

Step 2

Copy your games

Make a folder on your computer like this, with your games inside:

roms/
  gb/    Game Boy games (.gb)
  gbc/   Game Boy Color games (.gbc)
  nes/   NES games (.nes)

Then choose that roms folder (or just pick some game files), and click Copy to badge. About 2 minutes per 1 MB.

    Step 3

    Play

    On the badge: open Retro, pick the console, pick the game, press SELECT.

    • SELECT = A, CANCEL = B
    • UP + DOWN together = START, LEFT + RIGHT together = SELECT
    • BOOT (on the back) = quit. Your game is saved.

    Early test build: Game Boy and Game Boy Color run at about half speed, NES is very slow.
