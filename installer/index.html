Game Boy, Game Boy Color and NES games on your Breakpoint 2026 badge. Two clicks, nothing to install on your computer.

BA
This browser can't talk to the badge. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer (Windows, Mac or Linux).

Before you start

  • A computer with Chrome or Edge.
  • Your badge with its microSD card inside, and a USB cable that carries data (some only charge).
  • Your games. None are included: use games you copied from your own cartridges, or free homebrew games.
Step 1 · recommended

Back up your badge

Saves everything on your badge (system, apps, settings, badge identity) as one 16 MB file on your computer, so you can go back to exactly this state later. Takes about 10-15 minutes.

Step 2

Install Retro

Plug in the badge, click the button and pick USB-SERIAL CH340. Your badge keeps its Wi-Fi, badge ID and store apps. Only the operating system is updated.

Step 3

Copy your games

Make a folder on your computer like this, with your games inside:

roms/
  gb/    Game Boy games (.gb)
  gbc/   Game Boy Color games (.gbc)
  nes/   NES games (.nes)

Then choose that roms folder, and click Copy to badge. About 2 minutes per 1 MB.

Or pick single games, and which console they're for:

Console for single games
    Step 4

    Play

    On the badge: open Retro, pick the console, pick the game, press SELECT.

    • SELECT = A CANCEL = B
    • UP + DOWN together = START, LEFT + RIGHT together = SELECT
    • BOOT (on the back) = quit. Your game is saved.

    Link cable Trade or play with a friend: both pick each other under Link cable, check that both screens show the same 6-digit code, then press A.

    Early test build Game Boy and Game Boy Color run at about half speed, NES is very slow.

    Go back to how it was

    Choose a backup file from step 1 to put the badge back exactly as it was then, including the original system. Games on the microSD card stay.
